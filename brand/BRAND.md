# Brand guidelines

A lightweight brand sheet so AURA looks consistent across the README, pitch, and site.

## Name & tagline

- **Name:** AURA (always uppercase)
- **Full name:** AURA — Autonomous SRE Platform
- **Tagline:** *Reliability without humans. Safety without compromise.*
- **One-liner:** AURA detects, diagnoses, and heals cloud incidents in real time — and proves every
  AI fix in a sandbox before it touches production.

## Positioning statement

> For platform and SRE teams drowning in 3 AM pages, AURA is an autonomous SRE teammate that resolves
> incidents end-to-end. Unlike read-only AI copilots that only recommend, AURA safely *acts* — proving
> every fix in a sandbox (Shadow Mode) before production ever sees it.

## Voice

- **Confident, not boastful.** Show the work; label what's real.
- **Honest by default.** We publish our limits. Trust is the product.
- **Technical, not jargon-soaked.** Speak to engineers like peers.

## Logo mark

The mark is a circular "aura" ring with a focused core — intelligence surrounded by a protective
boundary. See [`logo.svg`](logo.svg).

⚕️ healing · 📝 audit · 💰 FinOps · ☁️ cloud.
